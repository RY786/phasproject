1) The Seer: Reveals one upcoming danger on the board, allowing the ghost to avoid it early.
2) The Warden: Grants immunity to one trap (e.g. salt circle or crucifix), shielding the ghost.
3) The Whisperer: Silences noise briefly, letting the ghost pass through a high-risk area undetected.
4) The Riftwalker: Teleports the ghost to a previously visited safe tile, skipping over obstacles.
5) The Hollow Monk: Slows down enemy investigators for one turn, giving the ghost breathing room.
